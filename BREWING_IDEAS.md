# Ingredient Ideas

Ingredients for the cauldron brewing system, drawn from Nordic, Celtic, Welsh, Arabic, and Egyptian mythologies.

## Implemented

| Ingredient | Origin | Mythological Property | Game Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lavender** | Celtic | Calming, spiritual clarity | Mana regeneration |
| **Mugwort** | Norse/Celtic | Psychic powers, trance states, seidr magic | Spell power |
