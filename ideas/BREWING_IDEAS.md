# Ingredient Ideas

Ingredients for the cauldron brewing system, drawn from Nordic, Celtic, Welsh, Arabic, and Egyptian mythologies.

## Implemented

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Lavender** | Celtic | Calming, spiritual clarity | Mana regeneration | Purple (0.60, 0.40, 0.80) |
| **Mugwort** | Norse/Celtic | Psychic powers, trance states, seidr magic | Spell power | Dark green (0.30, 0.50, 0.20) |
| **Yarrow** | Norse | Wound healing (Thor's mother used it on him) | Defender healing (5 HP/s) | Cream white (0.95, 0.90, 0.75) |
